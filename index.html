    How to use this
    • Open this file in Chrome or Edge and allow the microphone when asked. Set the device on the music stand, in a quiet room.
    • Play firmly and let the sound ring. The page moves on when it hears the right note (right key, right octave) or the right chord.
    • An older upright may be a little out of tune. Press Tune: play middle C once and the page adjusts.
    • Hint points you to a landmark or the bottom note of a chord. Show me lights up the keys. Either one means it doesn’t count as a first try.
    • Read from landmarks instead of counting up from C. Next line or space = the next white key. Line to line, or space to space = skip one white key.
    • Chords: a root-position triad is three notes stacked on all lines or all spaces. Inversions move the bottom note to the top, so the root sits just above the wider gap.
    • Metronome: start slow (50–60) with “Keep going: every 4 beats.” Speed up only when most notes are green. The click is set to a pitch the page ignores, but headphones help if it confuses the listening.
